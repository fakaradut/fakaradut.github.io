Fluffytail: Squirrel Adventure

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short

  • The game collects no personal data from anyone, children included.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no chat. The game does not connect to the internet.
  • Progress and settings are saved only on your device, and you can delete them at any time.
  • The one optional purchase is handled by Apple or Google. We never see any payment details.

Who we are

Fluffytail: Squirrel Adventure (in Turkish Fluffytail: Sincap Macerası; "the game") is made by Moon Arc Works, an independent studio ("we", "us"). This policy covers the game on iPhone, iPad and Android, and these web pages. For anything about privacy, write to support@moonarcworks.com.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on our side, use, share or sell any personal data. In particular it:

  • never asks for a name, age, birthday, e-mail address, phone number, photo, voice or location, and has no place to type them;
  • has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no friends list, no leaderboards and no way to share anything with other players;
  • shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social-media or tracking software (SDKs) from anyone;
  • does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA or Android advertising ID) or any other device identifier;
  • does not connect to the internet: it sends nothing to us or to anyone else. The Android version does not even ask for the internet permission.

What stays on your device

To let your child carry on where they left off, the game saves a few things in its own private storage on the device:

  • progress: chapters reached, golden acorns, stars, costumes and stickers;
  • settings: music and sound volume, vibration and language;
  • whether the Full Adventure is unlocked;
  • pictures taken with the game's photo mode. They stay inside the game; they are not added to your photo library.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else, and none of it identifies your child. On iPhone and iPad, if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, Apple's backup may include the game's save under your own account; we have no access to it. The Android version is excluded from Google's app backup.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Saves stay on the device until you delete them. We keep nothing about you or your child, because nothing reaches us.

  • Start over: on the main menu, tap the gear (Settings), then the bin (Delete save), and answer the grown-up question. This erases all progress. The Full Adventure stays unlocked.
  • Delete everything: delete the game from the device. This removes the save, the settings and any photo-mode pictures. A purchase is never lost: it stays with your Apple ID or Google account and can be restored (see Support).

Since the game collects no personal data, there is no consent to give or take back. If you have ever e-mailed us, you can ask us to delete those messages at any time (see below).

Purchases

The first two chapters are free. One optional, one-time purchase, the Full Adventure, unlocks the rest. It is made through Apple's App Store or Google Play, and buying or restoring it first asks a grown-up question. Apple or Google handle the payment under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google). We never see your name, account, card or any other payment details; the game only learns from the store on the device whether the Full Adventure has been bought. Apple and Google send us sales reports that do not identify you.

Third parties

We do not share, sell or rent any data, and no third party receives data about you or your child from the game. The game is built with the open-source Godot engine and an open-source purchase library that only talks to the App Store or Google Play on the device; neither sends anything to us or to anyone else. Should this ever change, any partner would have to protect data at least as well as this policy and the Apple and Google rules for children's apps require, and we would update this policy and the store privacy labels first.

If you have turned on sharing with app developers in your device's settings, Apple or Google may show us anonymous crash and performance reports (for example the device model, the system version and where the game stopped). Apple and Google collect these under their own policies; they do not identify you or your child, and we use them only to fix bugs. You can turn this off at any time: on iPhone and iPad in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, and on Android in your device's usage and diagnostics settings.

Children's privacy

Fluffytail is made for children aged 6 to 8 and is meant for the App Store's Kids Category and Google Play's Families programme. We follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR rules for children's data, and similar laws such as Türkiye's KVKK. Because the game collects no personal information, it never needs to ask a child or a parent for personal details or consent.

A child cannot type in personal details, share pictures, talk to other players or leave the game by tapping a link. Buying, restoring a purchase, deleting the save and anything that leads out of the game sit behind a grown-up question (a multiplication meant for adults).

If you write to us

If you e-mail us, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you; we never add you to a mailing list or share your message. Our e-mail reaches us through our providers, Cloudflare (which forwards mail sent to our address) and Google (which hosts the mailbox), each under its own privacy policy. We delete support conversations when they are no longer needed, at the latest 12 months after the last message, and sooner if you ask. Children: please ask a grown-up to write to us for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, KVKK, COPPA or US state laws), you or your child's parent or guardian may ask what personal data we hold, have it corrected or deleted, object to its use or ask for a copy. For the game the answer is simple: we hold none. For e-mails you sent us, write to support@moonarcworks.com and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Saves are kept in the game's private storage, which the operating system shields from other apps. Payments run through Apple's and Google's own secure systems, and our e-mail travels over encrypted connections.

These web pages

moonarcworks.com uses no cookies, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare; like any web server, they may keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the site secure and running. The studio home page remembers which language you picked in your own browser; that never leaves your device.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. If we ever wanted the game to collect any data, we would change this policy and the store privacy labels before that update reached you, and ask for a parent's verifiable consent wherever the law requires it.
