Questions from parents

How do I restore the Full Adventure?

The game checks the store when it starts, so on a new device or after reinstalling, the Full Adventure usually comes back by itself. If it doesn't:

  1. Make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account that bought it, and is online.
  2. Open the game, tap the gear on the main menu (Settings), then the round arrow (Restore purchase).
  3. Answer the grown-up question.

Still locked? Write to us with the store receipt date (no card details, please).

Can the whole family use one purchase?

iPhone and iPad: yes. The Full Adventure supports Apple's Family Sharing, so family members in your Family Sharing group can restore it on their own devices with their own Apple ID, as long as purchase sharing is turned on.

Android: Google Play does not share in-app purchases through Family Library. The purchase works on every Android device signed in with the Google account that bought it.

What does the Full Adventure include? Is there a subscription?

The first two chapters are free. One single, one-time purchase unlocks the Full Adventure: chapters 3 to 16, with the Winter Land, the island, the city and the Moon. There is no subscription and nothing else to buy. The price is shown in the store in your currency, and payment is charged to your Apple or Google account.

How do I stop my child buying by accident?

Every purchase and restore first asks a grown-up question (a multiplication). You can also turn on Ask to Buy (Apple Family Sharing) or purchase approvals in Google Family Link, or require a password for purchases in the App Store or Google Play settings.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are made to Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them: on iPhone and iPad at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android from your order history in Google Play (Google's refund help). We can't refund purchases ourselves, but we are happy to help if something went wrong.

How do I delete the save and start over?

On the main menu, tap the gear (Settings), then the bin (Delete save), and answer the grown-up question. All progress is erased and the game starts from the beginning. The Full Adventure stays unlocked. Deleting the game from the device removes everything it saved.

Does it need the internet?

No. The game plays fully offline, on a plane or in the car. Only buying or restoring the Full Adventure needs a connection to the App Store or Google Play.

Which languages does it speak?

34 languages. The game starts in your device's language; to change it, tap the gear (Settings) and then the globe.

Which devices does it run on?
  • iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
  • Android phones and tablets with Android 10 or later and a 64-bit processor.

It is a 3D game, so newer devices run it more smoothly. It is played in landscape.

Are there ads? Does the game collect data?

No ads, no tracking, no accounts and no chat, and the game collects no personal data. Saves stay on the device. Read the full privacy policy.

My child is stuck. What now?

If a puzzle takes too long, a little bluebird flies over to show the way, and an arrow on screen always points to the next goal. Running out of hearts is fine too: the game carries on from the last checkpoint.
