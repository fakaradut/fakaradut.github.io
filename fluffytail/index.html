Fluffytail: Squirrel Adventure

One stormy night, Redtail the fox steals the golden acorns and carries little Peanut away in his sack. Run, climb, double jump and glide on your fluffy tail through five worlds, all the way to the Moon, to bring your sister home.

What's inside

Five worlds, sixteen chapters

The hazelnut hills, the Winter Land, a sunny island, Baron Magpie's city of rooftops and, at last, a rocket ride to the Moon. Every chapter opens like a page of a storybook.

Friendly fights

Bosses with their own tricks, and nobody gets hurt: beaten foes get dizzy and run off, and many become friends. Out of hearts? Carry on from the last checkpoint.

Bring the little ones home

Penguin chicks, owlets, turtle hatchlings, kittens and bunnies are lost. Find them and they waddle after you, all the way home to their mom.

Never stuck

A little bluebird flies over to show the way when a puzzle takes too long, and an arrow always points to the next goal. Easy controls made for small hands.
