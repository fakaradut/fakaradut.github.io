For cats, dogs and their people

Your pet's health card, the nearest vet and a lost-pet kit. In one app.

Vaccine schedule and reminders, weight and nutrition, nearby vets with 24/7 clinics first, and a QR tag and poster if they ever go missing. No account. Your data stays on your phone.

What's inside

  • 💉

    Smart vaccine schedule

    Templates for cats and dogs. Mark a dose as done, skipped or not applicable, and the next date is worked out from the real day it was given.

  • ⏰

    Reminders

    Medicine, vet visits, grooming, food running out and birthdays, as gentle notifications at 9 in the morning.

  • 📍

    Nearby and emergency

    Vets, pet shops, groomers, hotels, parks and shelters near you. One tap shows the closest vets with 24/7 clinics first, with call and directions.

  • 🆘

    Lost-pet kit

    A printable poster, a message to share, and a QR tag for the collar. The details live inside the link, never on a server.

  • 🩺

    Pet assistant

    Pick the symptoms and get one of three answers: see a vet now, today, or watch at home. No diagnosis and no medicine doses, ever.

  • 🍫

    Is it toxic?

    Search foods and plants offline, with separate answers for cats and dogs.

  • 🥣

    Nutrition

    Daily calories, grams of food, meals and water from weight, age, neutering and activity, plus a warning when weight changes fast.

  • 📄

    Records and reports

    Weight chart, expenses, documents and notes. A PDF health report for your vet, and a backup file you keep.

No account. No server for your pet's data.

  • Everything you enter is stored on your phone.
  • Your location is used on the phone to sort nearby places and is never sent anywhere.
  • Anonymous usage counts and crash reports can be switched off in one tap.
  • Moving to a new phone? Export a backup file and restore it.

Questions

Does Petiverse replace my vet?

No. It helps you keep records, remember dates and decide how urgent something is. Medical decisions belong to your vet.

Where is my data?

On your phone. There is no account. You can export a backup file and keep it wherever you like.

Which vaccine schedule does it use?

Common core vaccines for cats and dogs, with the validity your vet tells you. You can change dates and add your own reminders.

Does the map work in my city?

Places come from OpenStreetMap and are refreshed every week. Coverage differs by city; always call ahead in an emergency.

Which languages?

Petiverse speaks 34 languages, including English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Español, Français, العربية and 日本語.
