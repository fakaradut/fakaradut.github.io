Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

In short

  • No account. Your pets, health records, photos and reminders are stored only on your phone.
  • Your location is used on the phone to sort nearby places. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Anonymous usage counts and crash reports help us fix the app. You can switch them off in Settings.
  • Ads appear only if you choose to watch one to unlock a feature once. Plus members see none.
  • We never sell data.

Who we are

Petiverse ("the app") is made by Moon Arc Works, an independent studio ("we", "us"). This policy covers the app on iPhone, iPad and Android and these web pages. For anything about privacy, write to support@moonarcworks.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter: pet profiles and photos, vaccines, reminders, weight, expenses, documents, notes, lost-pet reports and the contact phone you add for posters and QR tags. It is stored in the app's private storage. We have no copy and no way to see it. Deleting the app, or Settings › Delete all data, removes it.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your location to sort a list of nearby places that ships inside the app, and to open directions in your maps app. Your location is not sent to us or stored. If you prefer, pick your city by hand instead.

Pet assistant questions

If you type a question into the pet assistant, the text is sent over an encrypted connection to our small service on Cloudflare, which passes it to an AI model run by Cloudflare (Workers AI) and returns the answer. We do not store or log the questions, and nothing that identifies you is attached. Cloudflare processes the request under its own privacy policy. Please don't type personal details. The symptom checker and Is it toxic? work offline and send nothing.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which features help, the app counts events such as "vaccine logged" or "poster shared" with Aptabase, an analytics service hosted in the EU. Events carry the app version, system version, language and a random session number, but no name, no device or advertising identifier and nothing you typed. You can switch this off in Settings › Anonymous usage & crash data.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a report goes to Sentry (EU region): the error, the screen, the device model, system and app version. It contains no personal data, and reports are deleted automatically within 90 days. The same switch in Settings turns this off.

Ads

Petiverse shows no banners and no pop-up ads. Some Plus features can be unlocked once by choosing to watch a short video ad from Google AdMob. When you do, Google may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad interactions to deliver and measure the ad and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first; on iPhone the system asks before any tracking. You can change your choice in Settings › Ad privacy options. Plus members are never shown ads. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

Plus is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment under their own privacy policies; we never see your name, card or account. The app only learns from the store on your phone whether Plus is active.

Posters, QR tags and sharing

A lost-pet poster, message, sitter card or health report is created on the phone and shared only when you choose, with the app you choose. A QR tag holds the details you put on it (name, species, phone, a short note) inside the link itself. Scanning it opens a static page on our website that reads those details in the visitor's browser; the details are not sent to our server and we don't store them. Anyone who scans the tag can see them, so add only what you want a finder to know.

Backups

A backup is a file the app creates on your phone when you ask. It contains your records and photos and is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. We never receive it.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. No push service is used.

Map data

Nearby places come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) and are bundled with the app and refreshed with updates. When a map is displayed, the system map provider (Apple Maps or Google Maps) may receive standard technical data such as your IP address under its own policy.

Children

Petiverse is meant for pet owners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK or US state laws) you can ask what personal data we hold, and have it corrected, deleted or copied. We hold no personal data about you except emails you send us and the anonymous counts described above, which cannot be linked to you. Write to support@moonarcworks.com and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

If you write to us

We receive your email address and message, use them only to reply, and delete the conversation when it is no longer needed, at the latest 12 months after the last message.

These web pages

moonarcworks.com uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare, which may keep short-lived technical logs such as IP addresses to keep the site running.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above, and update the store privacy labels before an app update collects anything new.
