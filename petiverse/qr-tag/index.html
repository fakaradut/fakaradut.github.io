QR pet tag: whoever finds your pet can call you

The fastest way home for a lost cat or dog is the information it carries. A QR tag puts that information and your phone number on the collar, and no app is needed to scan it.

How it works

  1. Open your pet's lost kit in Petiverse and add your phone number, its looks and a short note.
  2. Print the tag sheet, cut it out and attach it, or have a tag maker print the QR.
  3. A finder scans it with the phone camera and sees your pet's name, looks and a button to call you.

What does the finder see?

Your pet's name, sex and age, whether it is microchipped, the looks and note you wrote, and a big call button. The page opens in the language of the finder's phone.

Where is the information kept?

On no server. The details travel inside the link in the QR, and the page shows them only on the phone that scanned it. If your number changes, print a new tag.

Tag tips

  • Keep it short: less text means a code that scans more easily.
  • Laminate the tag or use a clear tag holder.
  • Scan it with your own phone before you attach it.
  • A microchip does not replace a tag; both together are safest.
