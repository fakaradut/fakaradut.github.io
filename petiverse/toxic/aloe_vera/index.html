Is Aloe vera toxic to cats and dogs?

🐱 Cats

Risky

Risky. Small amounts may cause problems; avoid it and ask your vet if eaten.

🐶 Dogs

Risky

Risky. Small amounts may cause problems; avoid it and ask your vet if eaten.

My pet ate it. What now?

  1. Call your vet or the nearest 24/7 clinic. Say what was eaten, how much and when.
  2. Do not try to make your pet vomit unless a vet tells you to.
  3. Keep the packaging or a photo of the plant to show the vet.
  4. Watch for vomiting, drooling, weakness, shaking or trouble breathing.

This is a quick screening list, not medical advice. When in doubt, assume it is toxic and call a vet.
