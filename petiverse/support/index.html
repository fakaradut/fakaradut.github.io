Support

Can't find an answer? Write to us; we read every message.

Moving to a new phone

On the old phone: Settings › Export backup, and save the file to Drive, iCloud or email. On the new phone, open Petiverse and tap “I have a backup, restore it”. The file includes photos.

Restoring Plus

Open Settings › Petiverse Plus and tap “Restore purchases”, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by the store: on iPhone in Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

Reminders don't arrive

Allow notifications for Petiverse in your phone's settings (Settings › Notification settings in the app takes you there). On some Android phones, also turn off battery optimisation for Petiverse.

The QR tag

The tag opens a page that shows only what you put on it: name, species, phone and a short note. The details live inside the link; we don't store them. To change them, make a new tag.

A place is missing or wrong

Places come from OpenStreetMap, which anyone can improve at openstreetmap.org. Changes reach the app within about a week.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete all data removes everything from the phone. We hold no copy of your pet's data, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Contact

Email support@moonarcworks.com. Please include your phone model and the app version (Settings › About).
